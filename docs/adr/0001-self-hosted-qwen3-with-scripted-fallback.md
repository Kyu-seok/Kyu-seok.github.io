# Self-host Qwen3 on the CodeBite mini PC, with the scripted chat as the permanent fallback

The portfolio chat was fully scripted (keyword matcher, authored prose, static GitHub Pages). We decided to answer free-typed questions with a Qwen3 model (1.7B or 4B, chosen by measurement) served by llama.cpp `llama-server` on the Intel N100 mini PC that runs CodeBite, behind a thin Go gateway at ask.code-bite.com. The point is the showcase: the model running next to production on a 4-core, no-GPU box is the portfolio piece, so a hosted API (cheaper, faster, better Korean) was rejected on purpose.

## Considered options

- **Hosted API (Claude, OpenAI).** Better answers, no operations. Rejected: it demonstrates nothing about Kyu, and the answers were already fine.
- **RAG over the content files.** Rejected: the corpus is under 3,000 tokens and fits in one cached system prompt; revisit if the fact sheet outgrows that.
- **Ollama.** Rejected in favor of `llama-server`: Ollama unloads idle models and drops the cached fact-sheet prefix, and prefix caching is what makes a 4B model usable on this CPU (prefill is roughly 25 to 40 tokens per second).
- **Give the chat CPU priority during gradings.** Rejected: CodeBite is production; the model runs at a low CPU share and the page falls back to scripted prose.

## Consequences

- The page must never depend on the model: intent and card come from the keyword matcher in the browser; a 4-second first-token budget decides scripted vs generated prose; a health probe on first interaction skips the model when it is down.
- Chips stay scripted; only free text reaches the model. Thinking mode is off. Replies are single-turn.
- 2026-11-07 amendment after the spike (`server/spike/RESULTS.md`): Qwen3-4B failed the latency gate and Qwen3-1.7B's Korean was not good enough, so only English free text is generated; Korean free text stays scripted. Qwen3-1.7B Q4_K_M ships.
- 2026-11-08 amendment: Korean free text is generated after all, by Kyu's decision over the quality bar. A second measurement with a 합니다체 voice section in the fact sheet (`server/spike/RESULTS.md`, "Korean gate, second pass") passed the latency gate but, by the agreed quality bar (10 of 12 answers Kyu would say himself, the three trap questions declining without inventing, no answer copied verbatim from an example), did not pass: answers invented details and slipped register. Kyu chose to ship it anyway to see how it behaves on real visitor questions, with Korean questions capped at 40 characters (Korean is about one token per character, so longer questions miss the first-token budget) and Korean answers at 120 tokens. The page says this plainly in the "how does this chat work" reply. The question log is the review: if real Korean answers stay below the bar, Korean goes back to scripted.
- 2026-11-08 amendment, small talk, shipped by Kyu's decision over the quality bar: the model may make small talk (greetings, jokes, boredom, questions about the chat) in a voice section Kyu wrote, and voice only the opinions written in the fact sheet. It still adds no facts and declines out-of-scope questions (code, translation, general knowledge, people, politics). The model has no tools, so prompt injection can only make it say things; the defense is the prompt's "never" list, the link filter, the generated label, and the question log. A higher temperature for unknown-intent questions was measured and rejected. A 12-question small-talk bench (`server/spike/RESULTS.md`, "Small-talk gate") passed latency only after the sheet was cut back to about 2,200 tokens (prompt length sets generation speed on this CPU), and then failed the quality bar on both prompt variants while regressing the Korean declines the live prompt gets right. Claude's recommendation on record was scripted small talk through the matcher instead. Kyu shipped the better variant (run B, decline examples kept) to watch real questions, with a revert as the exit; the question log review is due 2026-11-22 or after 50 unknown-intent questions. PRODUCT.md principle 1 was amended from "every sentence" to "every factual claim".
- 2026-11-12 amendment, Korean back to scripted: the review the 2026-11-08 amendment named ran early on the question log (16 Korean entries to 2026-11-10, three of them the deploy smoke question). Two or three answers were acceptable; the rest slipped register (내가, 반말, an emoji), invented stories, pasted an example onto an unrelated question, or contradicted the page ("이 채팅은 제 개인적인 이야기를 나누는 데 사용됩니다"). Korean free text, and any question containing Hangul, is scripted again on the page and refused by the gateway (422); the 40-character cap and 120-token Korean limit are gone with it. A follow-up prototype let the model only pick one of Kyu's written Korean answers by id under a grammar: 12 of 28 against a bar of 24, misses all `none`, 19 of 28 with confident wrong picks when forced (`server/spike/picker/RESULTS-picker.md` on branch `prototype/ko-picker`). Next is one time-boxed prototype with a multilingual embedding model picking from the same answer bank; if it fails too, Korean stays scripted.
- Roughly 1.5 to 3 GB of RAM on the mini PC is held permanently by the resident model.
- A public, unauthenticated endpoint lives on the production box: one generation at a time, queue of 3, 10 questions/min/IP, 300-char questions, 220 output tokens, 25 s wall clock, Cloudflare in front, gateway on its own Compose project and network.
- Every question is logged permanently (JSONL on a volume, mirrored nightly to a private repo, shipped to Loki for dashboards only). PRODUCT.md's "no analytics" now applies to the page, not the gateway, and the page discloses the log.
- The measurement spike is a go/no-go: if even Qwen3-1.7B misses the gate on an idle box, the idea is dropped rather than the budget loosened.
